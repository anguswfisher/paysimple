Missing auto liability insurance requirement High
Contract requires CGL and workers' comp but does not specify automobile liability coverage. Standard AIA A101 Exhibit A typically requires auto liability with a minimum of $1,000,000 combined single limit. Without this, the owner may reject your insurance certificate.
Expected in: A101 Exhibit A — Insurance and Bonds, Section A.2.1
ⓘ Add auto liability requirement or confirm with owner that it's intentionally omitted
No late payment interest rate specified High
A201 §9.7 allows the contractor to stop work after 7 days' notice if payment is late, but the contract does not specify an interest rate on overdue payments. Without a stated rate, you may have limited recourse for the time-value cost of delayed payments.
Missing from: Article 5 — Payment, expected per A201 §9.6.1
ⓘ Negotiate adding interest rate (typical: prime rate + 2% or 1.5%/month)
Subcontractor lien waiver timing unspecified Medium
Contract requires lien waivers from subcontractors for prior period amounts, but does not specify how many days before the payment application these must be collected. This commonly causes payment delays when subs are slow to return waivers.
Section 9.3.2 — Lien waiver requirements
ⓘ Establish internal deadline (recommend: 5 business days before application due date)
No stored materials payment provision Medium
The contract does not address payment for materials stored off-site. For a $4.25M project, prefabricated components, steel, and specialty equipment often require advance purchase. Without this provision, these costs can't be included in progress payment applications, straining cash flow.
Missing from: Article 9 — expected per A201 §9.3.2
ⓘ Request amendment allowing stored materials with proof of insurance and title transfer
Owner financing verification not exercised Low
A201 §2.2 gives the contractor the right to request evidence of the owner's ability to pay. This right exists in the contract but there's no indication it has been exercised. For a $4.25M project, confirming financing is prudent.
