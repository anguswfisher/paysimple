Article 4 — Contract sum
4.1 The Owner shall pay the Contractor the Contract Sum of Four Million Two Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($4,250,000.00) in accordance with the terms set forth below, subject to additions and deductions as provided in the Contract Documents.
Article 5 — Payment
5.1.1 Based upon Applications for Payment submitted to the Architect by the Contractor and Certificates for Payment issued by the Architect, the Owner shall make progress payments on account of the Contract Sum to the Contractor as provided below.
5.1.2 The period covered by each Application for Payment shall be one calendar month ending on the 25th day of the month.
5.1.3 Payment shall be made by the Owner not later than thirty (30) days after the Architect issues a Certificate for Payment.
5.1.5 Each Application for Payment shall be based on the most recent schedule of values submitted by the Contractor in accordance with the Contract Documents.
Section 5.1.7 — Retainage
5.1.7.1 For each progress payment made prior to Substantial Completion, the Owner may withhold ten percent (10%) retainage from the approved amount.
5.1.7.2 When the Work is fifty percent (50%) complete, and if the Architect determines the Work is progressing satisfactorily, retainage shall be reduced to five percent (5%).
5.1.7.3 Retainage shall be released upon Substantial Completion, subject to a holdback of 150% of the estimated cost to complete punch list items.
Section 9.3 — Lien waivers
9.3.1 As a condition of payment, the Contractor shall provide conditional lien waivers with each Application for Payment and unconditional waivers covering previously paid amounts.
9.3.2 The Contractor shall submit lien waivers from all subcontractors and material suppliers for amounts included in the prior Application for Payment.
Section 11 — Insurance
11.1.1 The Contractor shall purchase and maintain commercial general liability insurance with a per-occurrence limit of not less than $2,000,000 and a general aggregate limit of not less than $4,000,000.
11.1.2 The Contractor shall maintain workers' compensation insurance as required by state law and employer's liability with limits of $1,000,000.
